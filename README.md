Tesseract has rebranded to **Fuse Energy** — `tesseractenergy.xyz` now 301-redirects to
`fuseenergy.com`. Fuse Energy is a UK-based, vertically integrated energy supplier founded
by former Revolut executives, delivering gas, electricity and EV charging to residential and
business customers. It raised $78M led by Balderton Capital and Lakestar.

Surfaced in the API Evangelist network as a portfolio-company lead. As of enrichment
(2026-07-21) Fuse Energy publishes only a consumer web/mobile app and **no public developer
API, SDK, or documentation** (`/developers`, `/api`, `/docs`, and all `/.well-known/*`
discovery paths return 404).
